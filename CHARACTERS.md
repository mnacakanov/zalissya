# CHARACTERS.md: who is who in Zalissya

One short entry per character, so that everything written about them agrees. **Check here before writing any line, quest or drawing that involves someone**, and update the entry when an owner decision changes them. Voices are also summarised in `CONTENT_GUIDE.md` (Writing); this file is the fuller reference.

- **Age** is given where the game shows it: in a costume (a vinok and a braid mean an unmarried girl, a kerchief a married woman, grey hair and long moustaches age), in a line of dialogue, or by an owner decision. "Not set" means nothing has fixed it yet; decide with the owner before fixing it in text.
- Names: Ukrainian / English locale (rule 1 in `CLAUDE.md`).

---

## The kobzar (the player)

- **Кобзар / the kobzar.** A young wandering minstrel with a kobza on his back. Male in Ukrainian. Called «кобзарю», «співаче» by peers, «синку» by the old. His power is songs, not weapons.
- **Look:** embroidered shirt, brown svytka, sharovary, a straw brryl.

## The khutir

### Myroslav (Мирослав), the otaman
- **Age:** old. Grey hair, the oseledets, grey moustaches. An old Zaporozhian from the Sich.
- **Look:** red zhupan with gold trim, blue sharovary, a sash, his lyulka.
- **Voice:** gruff and fatherly; orders that are really care. Calls the kobzar «синку».
- **Hurts:** buried his mother at seven in a plague a chumak caravan brought; hence he bars the road to the sick chumaky. No tobacco since spring.

### Vesna (Весна), the znakharka
- **Age:** **young**, an unmarried young woman (owner decision). Her vinok with ribbons and single braid say so; dark brown hair.
- **Look:** plakhta, a green kersetka, an apron, the vinok, a braid.
- **Voice:** dry and warm, practical, sees through everyone. Calls the kobzar «кобзарю» or «співаче», as an equal, never «синку».
- **Ties:** learned herbs as a girl from **Did Prokip**, until one Kupala night when she said it would rain, he said it wouldn't, and it poured. He never admitted it; she took her herbs and went to heal on her own. They have feuded, fondly, ever since. Calls him "old fool".
- **Hurts:** could have saved Ostap and didn't walk the three hundred steps to the camp when the otaman forbade it (if Ostap dies).
- **Does:** heals folk and cattle, brews tonics at her rack, teaches the lullaby, weaves the wreath against Viy's gaze.

### Radek (Радек), the smith
- **Age:** not set; a grown man in his prime (dark hair).
- **Look:** a leather apron over a grey shirt, a Polish cap.
- **Voice:** a Pole who ran from his lord; swears in Polish («пся крев», «Jezus Maria», «a nie…») mixed into his Ukrainian.
- **Hurts:** was once kept outside a khutir's fence himself.
- **Does:** the forge; forges the iron ward; buys horseshoes for chervintsi.

## The korchma on the chumak road

### Odarka (Одарка), the shynkarka
- **Age:** about thirty (owner decision). The math: Hetmanate village women married at about 19 (Serdiuk's parish data, 18th century), a few years of marriage before Stepan rode to the Sich, then seven years of waiting. A married woman's kerchief, dark hair. Older and steadier than Vesna; not old.
- **Voice:** sharp-tongued, counts everything.
- **Hurts:** her husband **Stepan** rode to the Sich seven years ago, "back by Pokrova", and never came back. She never collects Okhrim's debt, hoping someone feeds Stepan the same way. The **perelesnyk** comes to her in Stepan's shape.

### Okhrim (Охрім), the old Zaporozhian
- **Age:** old. Grey hair, oseledets, moustaches to the chest.
- **Voice:** tall tales, real scars.
- **Hurts:** the last of his kurin: of a hundred and forty, he alone came back. Counts them every evening. A friend of his looked at Viy.

## Out in the land

### Did Prokip (дід Прокіп), the vorozhbyt
- **Age:** old. Wild white hair, a beard to the belt.
- **Look:** a long patched svyta, a crooked blackthorn staff. Lives alone in a mossy khatka in the south-east forest with a black cat and a raven.
- **Voice:** grumbling; insults with love; calls the kobzar «шмаркачу» (snot-nose); never admits he is glad you came, or that it rained.
- **Ties:** Vesna was his apprentice (see Vesna). Forty years of handing every visitor a sieve.

### Hnat (Гнат) and Ostap (Остап), chumaky
- **Hnat:** grey-haired chumak leader, tar on his shirt, hauling salt from the Crimea.
- **Ostap:** the youngest chumak, a lad; burning with marsh fever on day 2.

### Taras Dovhyi (Тарас Довгий), never seen
- Planted the tobacco field, rode to the Sich, never came back. His empty khata (the pustka) keeps his letter.

## The Serpent's Cave and the tunnel

- **Onufriy (Онуфрій):** old; once a monk, now a hermit who keeps a candle at the cave's mouth. Quiet and kind ("Softly, son").
- **Omelko (Омелько):** a blind old kobzar in the cave's east nook; hears who you are in your step; calls you "brother".
- **Zmiy (Змій):** the old serpent asleep on a dowry-hoard in the west lair. Old and tired, dry humour ("I haven't eaten anyone in a hundred years. You're not tasty."). Sung to sleep with a sad song.
- **Vohnyk (Вогник):** a little wandering flame over the pool; bright, vain, forgets its own name.
- **Sydir (Сидір):** a chumak turned smuggler, camping in the tunnel; carries salt past other men's tollgates.

## Spirits

- **Domovyk (Домовик):** a little shaggy grandad in bast shoes, beard to the floor, living behind the pich of the kobzar's khata. Grumbling, house-proud, "a hundred winters" old.
- **Lisovyk (Лісовик):** tree-tall, bark for skin, a moss cloak, antlers, amber eyes. Lord of the pines and of the lost. Calls you "two-legs".
- **Chort (Чорт):** Gogol's devil: tailcoat, red waistcoat, pig's snout, little horns, goat legs, a tail. A vain, chatty dandy and merchant of souls, offended by bad manners. Paces by the old millstone.
- **Bolotianyk (Болотяник):** a frog-faced old man of the bog, draped in weed. Slow and cold, speaks in «…», calls you "dry-foot" («сухоногий»). Lives in his boloto past the river.
- **Viy (Вій):** squat and earth-black, roots for arms, an iron face, lids that hang to the ground. Asleep in the north-east hollow. Not a conversation: narration only.
- **Perelesnyk (Перелесник):** a fiery serpent that flies to those who grieve too long, in the shape of the one they lost. Comes to Odarka at night as Stepan.

## Lysa Hora

- **Beelzebub (Вельзевул):** host of the sabbath. Speaks in CAPITALS, ceremonious, keeps his word. Sleeps by day in his cave on the slope.
- **Solokha (Солоха):** the witch from Dykanka, a widow, grey-haired. Hides by day in the windmill.
- **Motrya (Мотря):** a dark-haired witch who knows where the nichnyky bloom. Hides by day in the hollow oak.
- **Opanas (Опанас):** a warlock with a goat's beard and a tall kuchma, "a witch with a moustache"; milks other people's cows. Hides by day under the steppe kurhan.
- **The dancers:** witches and warlocks circling the sabbath fire. Playful menace.

## Animals (they talk once Did Prokip spits in your ear)

- **The otaman's dog:** loves anyone who pets it, sees everything, nobody asks it.
- **The geese of the pond:** gossips; never trust a goose.
- **The cow, Krasulya (Красуля):** thinks about grass, doesn't lie (can't be bothered).
- **The horse at the korchma rail:** hauled firewood all its life, likes being called a Cossack horse, saw the fire over the korchma.
