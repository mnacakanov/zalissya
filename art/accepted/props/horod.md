# horod

- **Status:** ACCEPTED by the owner, 2026-10-31 ("they look beautiful").
- **Source:** BG2 / Icewind Dale set from the graphics demo (Issue #2), generated by Harry (Muse) as one asset sheet on flat `#00FF00`, then chroma-sliced and de-spilled into this transparent PNG.
- **What:** a small horod (kitchen garden): a diamond/isometric plot of dark tilled soil in rows — cabbages, beets, carrots, onions — with a low wattle fence along the two far edges.
- **Anchor:** bottom centre, at the plot's near corner.
- **Notes:** one fixed planting layout; seasonal/harvest variants would be separate paintings (BG2-style baked art).
